SECTION 3 – INTENDED USE: NOT FOR PROFESSIONAL ENGINEERING USE

3.1 ImpressionFEA is an educational and conceptual tool. It is intended
for teaching and learning closed-die forging, for demonstrations,

thickness, temperature, friction, or ram speed affect die fill and
press load.

3.2 The Software rests on substantial simplifying assumptions. It
assumes isothermal forming (no die chilling and no heat generated by
deformation or friction), a rigid-viscoplastic material (no elasticity,
springback, or residual stress), rigid dies (no deflection, wear, or
temperature change), constant shear friction, and quasi-static forming
at constant ram speed. Material data are representative or fitted to
published tables and are not certified. Results depend on the element
size and other settings. The mesh check, warnings, and benchmark
comparisons the Software provides are aids, not guarantees. Press
loads, die fill, stresses, strains, damage, defects, and other results
can differ substantially from those of a real forging.
